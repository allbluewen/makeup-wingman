# The Makeup Wingman

Your partner asks *"How's my makeup?"* or *"What do you like about it?"* and your brain freezes. **The Makeup Wingman** turns any AI into a 5-second wingman that spots real, specific details from a photo and translates them into natural, non-cringey compliments.

## Option 1: Install as an AI Skill (Claude / Gemini Skills)

1. Download or copy [`makeup-wingman/SKILL.md`](./makeup-wingman/SKILL.md).
2. Import the `makeup-wingman` folder into your platform's **Skills** manager.
3. Whenever you need help, just ask *"How do I answer 'how's my makeup?'"* or upload a photo with *"what do I say?"*

## Option 2: Quick Copy-Paste (ChatGPT / Gemini Gems / Claude Projects)

1. Open your AI app, create a new **Gem**, **Custom GPT**, or **Project** (or start a fresh chat).
2. Copy the instructions inside [`makeup-wingman/SKILL.md`](./makeup-wingman/SKILL.md) (everything below the second `---` line) and paste them into the **Instructions** box.
3. Snap a photo next time you're on the spot and pick one of the 3 options.

## Customization Tips
You can reply or add preferences anytime:
- **Style:** `Natural` (default), `Observant`, or `Romantic`
- **Occasion:** Date night, wedding, anniversary, everyday
- **No-Go Zones:** Any feature your partner is self-conscious about so the AI avoids mentioning it

## Privacy
Only upload photos with the subject's permission. The Skill itself does not contain code for storing or transmitting photographs. How uploaded images are processed and retained depends on the AI platform you use.
